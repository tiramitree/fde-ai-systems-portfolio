# AI Assistance and Ownership

## What this repository is

This repository contains AI-assisted rapid reference implementations built to
explore security, approval, observability, evaluation, and release-control
patterns for local AI applications. The examples use fictional data and are
intended for learning, review, and controlled local demonstration.

## What it does not prove

The repository is not evidence of:

- customer or employer deployment;
- production traffic, service-level objectives, or incident ownership;
- independent enterprise consulting;
- a production RAG, agent, or AIOps platform;
- customer adoption, revenue, or business impact.

Public descriptions must not convert these reference implementations into
those claims without separate, direct evidence.

## Human and AI responsibility

AI coding agents materially assisted with code, tests, documentation, and
repository maintenance. Public evidence should therefore focus on what can be
reproduced from the repository rather than implying that file volume or commit
count alone demonstrates independent authorship.

The human owner remains responsible for:

- deciding which claims are published;
- reviewing and accepting technical changes;
- verifying test and evaluation results;
- maintaining the repository's safety and evidence boundaries;
- explaining design choices and limitations.

## Appropriate portfolio wording

> AI-assisted reference implementations used to study permission boundaries,
> governed tool use, auditability, evaluation, and release controls in
> local-first AI systems.

This wording may be shortened, but its factual boundary must not be weakened.
